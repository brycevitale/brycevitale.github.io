Introduction

Hello, I’m Bryce. I built this website to use as a resume + project hub. I kept it simple and focused on clean structure and a solid UI.

Background

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from Fordham University and I completed my M.S. in Cybersecurity in May 2026.

I have been a Software Engineering intern at American IT Solutions since 2022, where I have gained hands on experiences with server infrastructure and full-stack application development.

In my free time I really enjoy building side projects. One of my longer term projects is an indexing platform for a startup called Myrtle Equities, where I have been designing and implementing backend services that ingest, store, and serve market data to private clients.

During my four years in undergrad I co-founded DormDirect, which is an inter-campus food delivery service. That experience taught me what it takes to build a product and a company from the ground up.

Interests

Outside of day to day development, I’m into quantum computing and what it means for modern encryption. I like the practical side of it: how companies actually migrate to post-quantum security.

I’m also a lifelong hockey player and a band frontman. Hockey taught me how to show up for a team. Music is how I reset.

What I focus on when I build
  • Semantic HTML and clean easy-to-follow code structure.
  • Performance: fast loads, minimal bloat, responsive layouts.
  • Security: least privilege and secure data handling.
  • Real deployment: projects should run outside my local development environment.
